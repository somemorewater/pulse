      What verification proves

      Given the revealed seed, the backend recomputes HMAC-SHA256(seed, match:sequence:market) and compares it against the stored commitment and value. A pass proves the published numbers were fixed before the seed was known.

      It does not prove the odds themselves are fair. The probability model is a separate, deliberately simple Poisson simulation; fairness here means deterministic reproducibility, not predictive edge.

      The seed appears only after the explicit reveal step — never in normal reads.
